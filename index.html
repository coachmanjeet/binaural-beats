How it works
Left ear plays one tone, right ear plays a slightly higher one. Your brain perceives a third tone, the difference. Example: 200 Hz left and 210 Hz right gives a 10 Hz beat. That beat only exists in your head.

Delta (0.5-4 Hz): deep sleep, rest
Theta (4-8 Hz): deep meditation, creativity, light sleep
Alpha (8-13 Hz): relaxation, light meditation, stress reduction, pre-sleep
Beta (13-30 Hz): focus, concentration, problem-solving
Gamma (30+ Hz): peak focus, flow state

Try 10 to 30 minutes at first and see how you respond. Experiences vary, and it does not suit everyone.
